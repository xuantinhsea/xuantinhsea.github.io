01About

Bridging research and practice in water resources.

I am a Senior Hydrologist in the Water Resources & Energy Department of Nippon Koei Co., Ltd. in Tokyo, working on water resources, coastal engineering and climate change adaptation. Over more than 18 years I have advised governments, international organisations and private-sector clients across the region.

My work covers project management, technical advisory, capacity building and policy development, on projects for ADB, JICA, the World Bank and national agencies. Before consulting I was an Assistant Professor at Tohoku University, and I still publish applied research and build open tools for flood forecasting and water data.

Coastal morphodynamics · Shoreline evolution · Tsunami modelling · Flood risk assessment · Hydrological & RRI modelling · Remote sensing · Machine learning

04Applications

Software I have built for flood forecasting, water data and coastal engineering.

Floods & water data

  • Global Water Atlas

    One map of observed water data worldwide — river discharge and level, rainfall, reservoirs and tide gauges from 29 providers and about 98,000 stations — with selection tools, previews and batch CSV download. Built on the open-source RivRetrieve-Python library.

    Python · FastAPI · React LiveCode

  • Magat Dam FloodCast

    Operator dashboard of an automated flood-forecasting system for the Magat River basin, Philippines. Forecast rainfall drives the ICHARM RRI model to give discharge, water level, inundation depth and warning levels. Published as an archived snapshot.

    RRI model · Leaflet · Chart.js LiveCode

  • Thai Hydro Watch

    Water level, discharge and rainfall from Thailand’s ThaiWater network — 359 water-level stations and 2,003 rain gauges — with automatic data-quality checks, hydrographs and CSV export.

    Python · JavaScript LiveCode

  • StormFlood Mapping Pro

    Google Earth Engine dashboard for rapid storm-flood mapping from Sentinel-1 SAR, following the UN-SPIDER method, with optical cross-checks and estimates of exposed population, cropland and built-up area.

    Google Earth Engine Code

Weather & climate

  • Open-Meteo Explorer

    Explore, chart and download global weather, marine, flood and climate-projection data: 7 data modes, 57 models and datasets and 128 variables, with export to CSV, Excel and PNG.

    React · Leaflet · Chart.js Code

  • Model Spread

    Sixteen weather models side by side on a phone, two weeks back and sixteen days ahead — showing how far forecasts disagree, which matters most before a flood.

    React · PWA Code

  • BestCast

    A local forecast that reconciles sixteen forecast services into one most-likely figure for each day and hour, in Vietnamese, English and Japanese.

    React · PWA LiveCode

  • Hydro-MetCO

    Desktop application for downloading and processing hydrological, meteorological, climate and ocean datasets from Copernicus, CHIRPS and GSMaP.

    Desktop Repository

Coastal engineering

  • CoastalEng Pro

    Eighteen coastal engineering calculators — wave dispersion, run-up, EurOtop overtopping, breakwater armour, tides, sediment transport, shoreline evolution and extreme-value analysis — modernised from the classic Dalrymple and Lynett coastal applets.

    Next.js · TypeScript Code

  • ASTRO-TIDE

    Global tidal analysis: pick any ocean point to extract predictions from FES2022, TPXO10, EOT20 and other tide models, with harmonic constituents, tidal datums and data export.

    React · FastAPI · pyTMD Code
